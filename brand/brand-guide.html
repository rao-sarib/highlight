Usage

✓ Do

  • Use the light app-icon tile as the primary mark.
  • Use the ink mark on light surfaces, the white mark on dark.
  • Keep clear space around the mark (≥ the width of one bar).
  • Keep the highlighter swipe at its angle — it's the brand cue.

✕ Don't

  • Don't recolour the gradient or change the bar order.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, or add drop shadows to the mark.
  • Don't straighten or remove the highlighter swipe.
  • Don't set the wordmark in a different font.
